# v2 overlap: months where both the top and bottom decile are observed

Date: 2026-11-17. Run on the v2 panel after the estimand was frozen in the [v2 protocol](2026-11-17-research-protocol-v2.md). The registered chronological forecasts (momentum and ridge) were trained in time order and the funds ranked into deciles each month. This report counts whether outcomes exist. **No return, spread or bound was computed or written.**

Command: `python -m src.models.overlap_report --database data/interim/panels/v2/fund_panel.duckdb --output-dir data/interim/overlap_v2`. Outputs (local, not in Git): `coverage_by_month.csv`, `coverage_summary.csv`. Code and panel hashes are in the protocol JSON under `freeze`.

## Mechanics checks

The run stops with an error if any check fails. All passed on the real panel:

- Decile weights sum to one in every model, month and decile.
- Every month has ten deciles.
- No training label became available after its decision date.
- The panel hash matched the registered v2 hash before anything ran.
- 60 tests pass, including tests that the coverage functions never read outcome values, that the 90% cutoff is inclusive, that missing weight is never filled with zero, and that the bounds widen as missing weight grows.

## Result

A decile counts as observed if at least 90% of its weight has an outcome. A month counts if both the top and bottom deciles are observed. There are 81 evaluation months (2019-02 to 2025-10).

| Model | Target | Months passing | Mean top observed | Mean bottom observed | Longest run of passing months |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ridge | Abnormal return | 80 of 81 | 98.0% | 97.1% | 46 |
| Ridge | Net return | 81 of 81 | 99.0% | 98.0% | 81 |
| Momentum | Abnormal return | 80 of 81 | 98.0% | 97.2% | 46 |
| Momentum | Net return | 81 of 81 | 99.0% | 98.0% | 81 |

- **The one failing month is 2022-12.** The bottom decile was 89.66% observed, just under the cutoff. The top decile was 99.3% observed. The same month fails for both models.
- **Lowest single-month coverage that still passes:** 90.5% (top) and 89.7% (bottom, the failing month) for abnormal return. For net return the lowest are 96.7% to 97.2% (top) and 93.2% (bottom).
- **The bottom decile is less observed than the top** for every model and target, by about one percentage point.
- **Coverage by year (ridge, abnormal return).** Top: 96.6% (2019), 97.1%, 98.4%, 98.9%, 98.7%, 98.4%, 98.0% (2025). Bottom: 95.5% (2019), 97.5%, 96.9%, 97.6%, 98.2%, 96.9%, 97.2%. Coverage is lowest in 2019 and does not trend sharply after.
- **The bootstrap requirement is met.** The inference rule needs a run of 12 contiguous passing months. The longest run is 46 for abnormal return and 81 for net return.

## What this means

- **The coverage failure from v1 is cleared under the new rule.** v1 had no month with both legs complete because any missing fund blocked a month. With a 90% rule on v2, nearly every month qualifies.
- **This is about coverage, not about bias.** Passing the 90% rule does not mean the observed funds represent the missing ones. The [risks report](2026-11-17-missing-outcome-risks.md) lists why they may not. The best/worst-case bounds are the check on that, and they have not been computed.
- **The bounds should be narrow in most months.** Mean missing weight is about 1% to 3% per decile, so the fill values move the leg return only a little. The range could still be wide in months near the cutoff, such as 2022-12.
- **The abnormal-return and net-return results rest on slightly different months.** Abnormal return has the one failing month. They should be reported separately, as registered.
- **Two predictors, almost the same coverage.** Ridge and momentum differ by less than 0.1 percentage points in mean coverage. I did not check whether the missing funds are the same funds under both rankings.
