# Milestones 2 and 3: objective and code review

The working research objective is to test whether fund flow and momentum predict next-month risk-adjusted fund returns under chronological training and conservative availability assumptions, then assess long-only value. Milestone 2 supplies a fund-level, availability-lagged panel and reconciles missing targets. Milestone 3 constructs monthly momentum and ridge rankings with past-only training and reports missing portfolio weights. Both are aligned with that objective as a reduced-information study; neither is a full replication or strict point-in-time reconstruction. D5 is not used.

The main unresolved issue is not code complexity: under the registered rule that any missing constituent outcome invalidates a portfolio month, neither model has an observed top-minus-bottom abnormal-return spread in any of the 81 months. The central predictive hypothesis therefore remains unanswered. The observed net-fund-return series is a class-aggregated proxy, not an investable retail portfolio; access, costs and passive comparisons remain Milestone 4 work. Missing outcomes are not random, and revised source vintages, incomplete groups and fund-name screening remain material limitations. Do not select a replacement missingness rule from the observed performance; document and version any new estimand and protocol before evaluating it, while retaining the v1 result.

Code review checked formation eligibility, available-label filtering for training and validation, training-only standardization, monthly deciles, ties and missing-weight handling. Synthetic tests cover delayed labels, validation outcomes that mature after tuning, preprocessing and future-source changes. The ridge tuning loop now constructs the training sufficient statistics once per validation month rather than once per candidate alpha. A full rerun matched the original run exactly in scores, selected alphas, fund ordering and deciles, and reproduced the portfolio coverage counts. No protocol, input or output rule was changed. The original protocol is retained as a historical registration with a later-status note.

This review does not independently verify the publication dates, source revisions, investor access or the correctness of every source classification. The historical target loadings use month-specific eligible fund compositions. Those constraints should remain visible in any eventual interpretation.
