# Fund panel validation and missing-target diagnosis

Date: 2026-11-16. This is a data-quality study, not a predictive-model or portfolio evaluation. It reads the v1 hash-verified panel and D2 files, never D5. The original common observation window is 1999-03 through 2025-12; the existing lagged panel starts in 1999-06 and extends through 2026-09. The primary experiment also requires targets to be assumed available by 2025-12-31. These are different boundaries.

Reproduce with `python -m src.data_processing.target_diagnosis --raw-dir /path/to/raw --output-dir data/interim/target-diagnosis-new`. The output directory must be empty, outside raw data. The program checks the v1 database, config and factor-file hashes and writes category totals, annual counts, objective coverage, comparison cohorts, overlaps and mapping/fee sensitivities. The database is opened read-only. The checked-in [annual target reasons](2026-11-16-target-reasons-by-year.csv), [coverage by year and objective](2026-11-16-target-coverage-by-year-objective.csv), and [evaluation-eligible monthly coverage](2026-11-16-evaluation-eligible-coverage.csv) are generated by that command.

## Formation rows and targets

Categories are mutually exclusive and ordered as shown. A case with multiple issues is assigned to the first applicable category. Thus these are attribution counts, not isolated causal effects or estimates of how many observations a particular fix would recover. Late labels are separated before other reasons. `Missing after cutoff` includes rows without a constructed target whose hypothetical target date would exceed the cutoff; it is not evidence that the future data exist. The cutoff uses the registered assumption of target availability at formation plus two month-ends.

The 297,858 nonmissing targets comprise 295,308 timely and 2,550 late; 368,572 are missing. The latter total comprises the 1,540 cutoff cases and all subsequent reasons. Across **all** formation rows, irrespective of precedence, 173,824 have at least one future positive-weight class with missing expense, and 353,456 have fewer than 30 valid target-history observations or no history count. The same row can appear in both counts or have another issue. A known excluded class exists in 24,088 fund-months, of which 13,353 have missing targets; this overlap does not establish that a partial group caused those missing targets. Unmapped classes cannot be assigned to an observed group, so this flag understates potential incomplete groups.

Among 2019-02 through 2025-10 formation months, 177,185 fund-months exist; 82,539 have both formation-time features, and 80,271 of these also have a target by the cutoff. Among the 82,539 feature-complete rows, 2,268 lack an abnormal-return target and 1,441 lack a next-month net fund return. Each of the 81 evaluation months has at least one feature-complete fund with a missing net return. The average monthly fraction missing is 1.76% of the *equal-weight eligible universe*, ranging from 0.12% to 4.72%. This is not the missing weight of a selected top-decile portfolio, which cannot be known before model rankings exist.

In this evaluation feature-complete cohort, the 2,268 observations with missing targets have median formation TNA 248.2, versus 512.9 for the 80,271 with targets, in the source TNA units. Average available formation expense is 0.00857 for missing-target rows (1,466 available) versus 0.00903 for observed-target rows (80,261 available). Fund-month observations are repeated over time; these comparisons describe selection, not causal effects or independent-fund samples. Overall timely coverage varies across objective labels: EDYG 111,431/246,913, EDYB 63,027/175,369, EDCS 62,271/119,939 and EDCM 37,193/80,711. These are group labels derived from selected classes, with `mixed` for conflicting class objectives; they are not a verification of economic fund style.

## Canonical working rules and validation

Use `src/data_processing/fund_panel.py` and its `read_factors` parser as the canonical working pipeline; retain its registered configuration and hashes. The other D2 loader and canonical parser produced identical months and values for all 1,197 overlapping source months. The panel rejects duplicate class/economic-month returns, normalizes trading dates to calendar month-end, applies the assumed lags before selection, and retains the exact selected class lineage. A synthetic test independently checks lagged-TNA weights, fund flow, expense timing, the return cutoff, rolling regressions and later objective changes. A further test changes future returns and adds a later group/objective record: earlier features, eligibility and already matured targets remain identical. This is evidence for the tested cases, not proof of historical publication or revision dates.

On the common formation window, the separate **upstream class-month** waterfall starts with 8,843,403 records: 2,261,158 selected; 2,191,821 first excluded by name; 2,027,273 by objective; 1,268,825 by missing group; 558,658 by no available summary; 360,283 by index flag; 152,759 by unknown objective; 21,583 by stale summary; 1,025 by exchange-traded flag; and 18 by ambiguous summary. These first-applicable, overlapping screens operate before fund rows exist and must not be added to the fund-month target reasons. The count differs from the earlier reconciliation's 8,827,991 because that comparison starts at 1999-04 to align with the retrospective source-date window; this diagnostic starts at formation 1999-03.

Keep the latest nonblank group **on the latest eligible summary row**, without carrying an older group over a blank. Of 1,268,825 class-months first excluded for a missing group, only 140 across 56 classes have a previous nonblank group both already available under the 90-day assumption and no more than 365 days old; 188 have a prior available group at any age. The older mapping's continued validity is not established. There were no changes in group ID between successive selected observations of a class in this snapshot, even across gaps; this does not verify unobserved changes or groups omitted for missing IDs. Full-file mapping uses future information and is not canonical.

The fee join can choose a newer turnover-only record instead of an older expense record. Among 425,770 selected class-months without a current expense, 1,126 have an older, available, nonnegative expense record within the 730-day limit. This is a **class-month** sensitivity, not the number of recoverable targets. Retain the registered rule for v1; changing it requires a documented new protocol and panel before evaluation. A reported net return plus annual expense/12 is only an approximation to gross return, and reported net returns must not be charged that expense again. The rolling factor regression requires 30 valid observations in the preceding 36 economic months and full rank; its intercept is estimated but not subtracted. Target betas use ex-post history and are not formation features. Historical fund returns in those regressions use the eligibility and aggregation observed for each historical month; they need not represent a fixed set of classes.

The objective hierarchy, exclusions for hedged/short styles and name/flag rules are the explicit working universe, not verified ground truth. For example, 82,009 of the name-first exclusions carry a domestic code that would otherwise satisfy the broad CRSP prefix rule. Names can refer to a fund family as well as the fund itself, so this screening is imperfect; these are not all verified false exclusions. Blank index and exchange-traded flags mean only “not flagged.” The panel does not use pull-date dead, delisting, end-date or inception fields as predictors or eligibility rules; fund and family ages remain unavailable.

## Outcome handling and remaining limitations

Keep all formation rows regardless of future outcomes. At portfolio construction, preserve the selected constituents and original weights, report the fraction of weight with missing realized net return and missing abnormal return separately, and mark the affected portfolio month unavailable instead of substituting zero or renormalizing. A closure or merger does not by itself imply a particular return or successor: use a reported valid return when present; otherwise record an unresolved missing outcome. Follow a successor or calculate liquidation proceeds only after an independently dated, defensible mapping and return are available. Report how this rule changes coverage and inference; the current extract does not establish investor access, successor proceeds, or tradability.

The 90-day summary lag, 180-day fiscal lag, monthly lags and staleness caps are assumptions, not verified disclosure timestamps. The supplied files are revised historical vintages, so this is an availability-lagged backtest rather than verified strict point-in-time data. The six duplicate summary keys have been conservatively excluded while ambiguous, but the original database has already retained one representative per key. The name screen, incomplete groups, high and uneven missingness, and absent independently verified fund-access and merger data remain material limitations. Review these before evaluation; changes to protocol v1 or its panel require a new dated protocol version and hashes before performance results are examined. No D5 data or model performance was used here.
